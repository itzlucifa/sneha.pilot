Featured piece · pp.18 — 25

"CARMESÍ — the cooks of Andalusia, continued."

This summer, the photographer Inés Llorca returned to Granada and Cádiz to photograph four cooks she had missed on the original 2025 shoot for Sublime Quarterly — and brought back, again, the colour pink.

These four frames have not been published before. They appear in ZINE.04, on the centre eight pages, printed in pink (PMS 806) and burgundy (PMS 188), on uncoated paper that takes the ink the way old hand-coloured photographs used to.

The piece is paired with a short prose essay by Hana Reza — first written in long-hand at a kitchen table in Cádiz — about whether or not, in the year 2026, one is allowed to make a project that is only about colour.

Words by Hana Reza · photographs by Inés Llorca · printed at Brila Printshop, Porto
