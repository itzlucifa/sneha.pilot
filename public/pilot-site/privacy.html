// Plain-Language Policy · Step 3 & 6

Plain PRIVACY. — zero legal jargon. Exactly what stays on your phone, and what leaves it.

Most AI apps stream your keystrokes and voice to a remote datacenter before doing anything. SNEHA is built to do the opposite: run locally on the phone in your hand.

1. What Stays on Your Device

The following data is stored solely in private Android app storage (/data/data/com.sneha.app) and never uploaded to any remote server:

  • ● Prompt & Conversation History: All conversation logs, task prompts, and contextual memory remain strictly in SQLite/local room databases encrypted by standard Android Keystore bindings.
  • ● Local Model Weights & Quantized Files: AI inference executes through local runtime engines on your device processor/NPU. Weights are stored locally on-device.
  • ● Automation State & Preferences: Scheduled local reminders, custom intent bindings, app settings, and tester preferences stay entirely on-device.
  • ● Android System Permissions: Any permission you grant (alarms, storage, calendar) is mediated entirely through the native Android OS permission broker.

2. What Leaves Your Device

Nothing leaves your device automatically. Data is transmitted only when you explicitly initiate an external action:

  • ● User-Initiated Pilot Sign-up: Submitting the pilot sign-up form sends your name, email, Android version, and device model to our intake endpoint to provision your pilot tester build.
  • ● User-Initiated Feedback Submissions: When you press "Submit Feedback" on this website or inside the app's feedback screen, the 1–5 rating, bug report notes, and device model are sent to our review intake endpoint.
  • ● Optional Cloud Fallback (If Enabled): If you intentionally configure and enable a personal cloud model key (e.g. Gemini API), requests pass directly from your client to that provider. By default, SNEHA requires no external cloud keys.

3. Third-Party Trackers & Analytics

Zero. In strict compliance with our Step 4, 5, and 6 engineering directives:

  • ● We do NOT use Google Analytics, Firebase Telemetry, Mixpanel, Segment, or Sentry.
  • ● We do NOT track advertising identifiers (AAID) or fingerprint device hardware.
  • ● We do NOT bundle proprietary crash-reporting daemons that phone home silently in the background.
  • ● We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or broker tester contact information under any circumstances.
